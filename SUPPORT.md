# Support & Community Help for LexiconUI

Thank you for using **LexiconUI** (`RootDeveloperDS/LexiconUI`)! Here is how to get help, propose new UI/UX terminology, or report issues.

---

## 1. Where to Ask Questions

- **UI/UX Terminology & Prompt Engineering Questions**:
  Use the **Vibe-to-Spec Translator** tab inside the app, or open a thread in [**GitHub Discussions**](https://github.com/RootDeveloperDS/LexiconUI/discussions).
- **Proposing a New UI/UX Concept + Live Demo**:
  Open the **OSS Contribute** tab inside LexiconUI to draft your concept, preview its live demo, and export the TypeScript/PR payload, or open a [**New UI/UX Concept Proposal Issue**](https://github.com/RootDeveloperDS/LexiconUI/issues/new/choose).

## 2. Reporting Bugs

If you encounter a rendering bug, broken sandbox slider, or layout issue:
1. Check existing [**GitHub Issues**](https://github.com/RootDeveloperDS/LexiconUI/issues) to see if it has already been reported.
2. Open a [**Bug Report**](https://github.com/RootDeveloperDS/LexiconUI/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) with your browser, viewport size, and steps to reproduce.
